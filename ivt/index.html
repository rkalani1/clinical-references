🚨 Absolute Contraindications Checklist

  • Prior Intracranial Hemorrhage: History of ICH, AVM, or aneurysm.
  • Ischemic Stroke or Head Trauma: Within past 3 months.
  • Active Internal Bleeding: Bleeding diathesis or severe coagulopathy.
  • Intracranial or Intraspinal Surgery: Within past 3 months.
  • Severe Uncontrolled Blood Pressure: SBP >185 or DBP >110 mmHg refractory to acute medication.
  • Platelets / INR: Platelets <100,000; INR >1.7; PT >15s; or LMWH within 24h.
  • Direct Oral Anticoagulants (DOACs): Use within 48 hours (unless normal activity tests or reversal available).

📈 Post-Thrombolysis Blood Pressure Targets

  • Target BP During/After IVT: Maintain blood pressure **< 180 / 105 mmHg** for at least 24 hours.
  • First-Line Antihypertensives:
    • Labetalol: 10-20 mg IV push over 1-2 min; can repeat.
    • Nicardipine: 5 mg/hr IV infusion, titrate up by 2.5 mg/hr every 5-15 min (max 15 mg/hr).
    • Clevidipine: 1-2 mg/hr IV infusion, double every 90 seconds (max 21 mg/hr).

⏱️ Post-Infusion Monitoring Schedule

  • Neurological checks & BP monitoring frequencies:
    • Every **15 minutes** for the first 2 hours.
    • Every **30 minutes** for the next 6 hours.
    • Every **1 hour** for the remaining 16 hours (total 24h).
  • Discharge Alert: Obtain emergency head CT/MRI immediately if patient develops severe headache, acute hypertension, nausea/vomiting, or worsening neurological exams.
  • No antiplatelets/anticoagulants for 24 hours until follow-up CT rules out hemorrhage.

🧠 Late-Window IV Thrombolysis Trials Summary

Trial Name Window Inclusion / Imaging Selection Key Clinical Findings & Outcome Data
WAKE-UP (NEJM, 2018) Wake-Up (Unknown) DWI-FLAIR Mismatch (ischemia on DWI, normal/no hyperintensity on FLAIR). Excellent 90-day functional recovery (mRS 0-1) was significantly higher with Alteplase vs. placebo (53.3% vs. 41.8%, p=0.02). No mortality difference.
EXTEND (NEJM, 2019) 4.5 – 9.0 hours Perfusion mismatch (CTP/MRP): Automated core-penumbra software showing salvageable tissue. Alteplase led to higher rate of mRS 0-1 at 90 days (35.4% vs. 29.5%, RR 1.44). Higher rate of symptomatic ICH (6.2% vs. 0.9%).
TRACE-III (NEJM, 2024) 4.5 – 24.0 hours Tenecteplase in late window select patients showing perfusion mismatch on automated CTP. Tenecteplase 0.25 mg/kg showed excellent functional outcomes and recanalization rates without excess safety events in tissue-profile selected patients.
