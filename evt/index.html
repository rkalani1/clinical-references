🧠 ASPECTS Scoring Key (MCA)

ASPECTS is a 10-point quantitative topographic score. Subtract 1 point for early ischemic changes (hypodensity, loss of gray-white ribbon) in:

  • C: Caudate head
  • P: Putamen
  • IC: Internal capsule (posterior limb)
  • I: Insular ribbon
  • M1: Anterior MCA cortex
  • M2: Lateral MCA cortex
  • M3: Posterior MCA cortex
  • M4: Anterior Sylvian (higher cuts)
  • M5: Lateral Sylvian (higher cuts)
  • M6: Posterior Sylvian (higher cuts)

🚨 Pre & Post-Procedural SBP Targets

  • Pre-Reperfusion: Maintain SBP **< 180 / 105 mmHg** (or < 220 mmHg if not thrombolyzed, but verify institutional parameters).
  • Post-Reperfusion BP Targets (based on TICI score):
    • TICI 2b/3 (Successful Reperfusion): Target **SBP <140 mmHg** (avoid intensive SBP <120 mmHg as this is associated with worse 90-day outcomes per ENCHANTED2-MT).
    • TICI 0/1 (Unsuccessful Reperfusion): Permit moderate hypertension (SBP target **<160-180 mmHg**) to maintain collateral flow.

⚡ Endovascular Clinical Trials Matrix

Clinical Domain Key Trial / Evidence Key Inclusions & Imaging Clinical Findings & Takeaway
Large Core LVO SELECT2 / ANGEL-ASPECT (2023) ASPECTS 3–5 or core volume ≥ 50 mL on CTP. Window up to 24 hours. EVT significantly improved functional independence (mRS 0-2 shifts) compared to medical management. Slight increase in sICH but net beneficial.
Basilar Artery LVO ATTENTION / BAOCHE (NEJM, 2022) Basilar occlusion up to 24 hours. BAOCHE selected based on pc-ASPECTS ≥ 6. Thrombectomy dramatically superior to medical therapy (mRS 0-3 at 90d ~30-45% vs 10-15%). Strict selection for basilar LVO up to 24h is highly standard.
Medium Vessel (MeVO) DISTAL / ESCAPE-MeVO (Ongoing) M2/M3 distal vessel occlusions. Routine endovascular thrombectomy is **NOT** supported for distal/MeVO occlusions due to higher perforation/dissection risk. Restrict to salvage.

📋 Triage & Selection Criteria

  • Early Window (<6 hours): Eligible if LVO on CTA (ICA or M1) and ASPECTS ≥ 6 (no advanced perfusion imaging required).
  • Late Window (6–24 hours): Criteria based on **DAWN** (clinical-core mismatch: age, NIHSS, and core volume on CTP) or **DEFUSE 3** (target perfusion mismatch: core <70 mL, mismatch ratio ≥ 1.8, and mismatch volume ≥ 15 mL).

References: [1] Sarraj A, et al. SELECT2. NEJM 2023. [2] Huo Y, et al. ANGEL-ASPECT. NEJM 2023. [3] Tao C, et al. ATTENTION. NEJM 2022. [4] Nogueira RG, et al. DAWN. NEJM 2018. [5] Albers GW, et al. DEFUSE 3. NEJM 2018.
