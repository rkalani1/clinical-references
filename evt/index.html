CT Perfusion & Collaterals Guide

Interpretation rules for automated perfusion maps (RAPID) and collateral flow assessments.

Perfusion Maps Definition

  • • Tmax >6.0 seconds: Defines critically hypoperfused tissue (penumbra + core). The primary metric for mismatch volume.
  • • rCBF <30% (Relative Cerebral Blood Flow): Used in RAPID to define the irreversibly damaged ischemic core.
  • • CBV (Cerebral Blood Volume): Reflects blood volume. Very low CBV indicates severe infarct core; maintained CBV suggests collateral compensation.
  • • MTT (Mean Transit Time): CBV/CBF ratio. High sensitivity for delays, but low specificity for infarct core.

Extended Window Selection (6-24h)

DEFUSE 3 (6-16h) • Core volume: <70 mL
• Mismatch volume: ≥15 mL
• Mismatch ratio: ≥1.8
• Tmax >6s: 15-180 mL
DAWN (6-24h) • Age ≥80: core <21 mL + NIHSS ≥10
• Age <80: core <31 mL + NIHSS ≥10
• Age <80: core 31-51 mL + NIHSS ≥20

Clinical CTP Pitfalls & Artifacts

• Crossed Cerebellar Diaschisis: Hypoperfusion of contralateral cerebellum from a large cerebral infarct mimic. Not an acute cerebellar core.
• Chronic Severe Carotid Stenosis: Baseline delay in Tmax >6s without acute stroke. Correlate with clinical timing and MRI DWI.
• Low Cardiac Output / Severe Stenosis: Bilateral global Tmax delays. All values elevated without matching a single vessel territory.
• Hyperacute Core Overestimation: In the first 60-90 minutes, RAPID can overestimate core size due to transient severe hypoperfusion. If high clinical mismatch is present, proceed with EVT.

Collateral Assessment Flow Grading

ASITN/SIR Grading System (0-4):
Grade 0: None Grade 1: Slow to <50% Grade 2: Slow to 100% Grade 3: Rapid to <100% Grade 4: Complete & Rapid
Grade 3-4 = Favorable collaterals; predicts slower core growth and better EVT outcomes.
Tan Score (Multiphase CTA, 0-3):
Score 0: No filling Score 1: Some filling Score 2: Delayed late filling Score 3: Normal pial filling
Used in ESCAPE trial. Score 2-3 indicates robust pial arterial collaterals.

Suspected LVO & Contrast Allergy Protocol

Who: Target Patient Population
Pt with a CT contrast allergy (mild, moderate, or unknown severity) AND suspected LVO
Excludes: any pt with known contrast related anaphylaxis (cardiovascular collapse).
Consent + Approval:
  • Whenever possible, consent pt and/or LNOK
  • Must have pre-administration approval from Stroke phone, Emergency MD, and Neuroradiology
  • Stroke phone will alert IR attending
Emergency Pre-Medication
Administer immediately prior to contrast study:
Hydrocortisone 200mg IV
— OR —
Methylprednisolone 40mg
Diphenhydramine 50mg IV
Communication & Documentation
  • Clear communication during stroke code re: plan and face-to-face discussion with ED team
  • Message plan in Pulsara and ensure reported during IR handoff
  • Must document in the Neurology note consent and include physicians who approved/agreed to plan.
