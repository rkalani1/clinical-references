Cerebral Venous Thrombosis (CVT) Guidelines

Anticoagulation in Hemorrhagic CVT

Anticoagulation is indicated for acute CVT even in the presence of pre-existing hemorrhagic venous infarction.

The hemorrhage is driven by venous congestion. Halting heparin allows thrombus progression and worsens bleeding.

Outpatient CVT Monitoring

  • Follow-up Scan: Repeat MRI/MRV or CTV at 3 to 6 months post-diagnosis to evaluate for venous recanalization.
  • Acute Decline: Repeat venous imaging immediately if there is new severe headache or focal deficits.
  • D-dimer: Serial D-dimer is NOT recommended for guiding treatment duration.

CVT: ICP & Seizure Management

1. ICP Assessment: Fundoscopy must be performed at baseline to monitor for papilledema. Formally check visual acuity and fields to detect optic nerve compromise.

2. Lumbar Puncture: LP opening pressure check is indicated if pseudotumor-like presentation is suspected (OP >25 cmH₂O).

3. Acetazolamide: Start 250-500 mg PO BID to lower CSF production if papilledema or progressive visual loss is documented.

4. Seizure Management: Routine prophylactic anti-seizure medications are NOT recommended. Treat acute symptomatic seizures immediately (e.g., Levetiracetam 500-1000 mg IV). Continue treatment for 3 to 6 months.

Inpatient Diagnostic Timelines & Management Guidelines

⚡ AIS Follow-Up Imaging Protocols

AIS Post-TNK:

  • 24 Hours: NCHCT required to rule out hemorrhagic transformation before antiplatelets.
  • 24-48 Hours: Brain MRI DWI/FLAIR to confirm infarct core and size.
  • Vascular: CTA head/neck or MRA to rule out large vessel disease.

AIS Post-EVT:

  • Immediate: Flat-panel CT in angiography suite to check for extravasation vs blood.
  • 24 Hours: NCHCT +/- CTA head to check patency and confirm recanalization.
  • 48-72 Hours: Brain MRI to evaluate final infarct volume.

🔴 Hemorrhage Follow-Up Imaging Protocols

Spontaneous ICH:

  • 6 Hours: NCHCT head to monitor for hematoma expansion (≥33% or ≥6 mL is significant).
  • 24 Hours: Repeat NCHCT if stable. CTA if spot sign or secondary vascular etiology suspected.
  • Subacute: Brain MRI (GRE/SWI) + contrast to screen for CAA or cavernoma.

Aneurysmal SAH:

  • Day 0: CTA head/neck to identify ruptured aneurysm. DSA if CTA is negative.
  • Days 3-14: Daily TCD measurements to monitor for vasospasm (peak risk Days 7-10).
  • Days 7-10: Repeat DSA or CTA head to rule out delayed spasm.

🧠 Cerebral Venous Thrombosis (CVT) Guidelines

  • Anticoagulation Rule: Therapeutic anticoagulation is indicated even in presence of hemorrhagic venous transformation (Class I). Venous congestion drives the hemorrhage; heparin treats the underlying thrombus.
  • ICP Control: Fundoscopy and visual checks at baseline. Treat papilledema with Acetazolamide 250-500mg PO BID.
  • Seizures: No prophylaxis. Treat active seizures immediately (e.g. Levetiracetam) and continue for 3-6 months.
  • Follow-up Scan: Repeat MRI/MRV or CTV at 3 to 6 months post-diagnosis to assess recanalization.
