DAPT Bedside Reference Card

Acute Non-Cardioembolic Ischemic Stroke & High-Risk TIA

⚡ Standard Bedside DAPT Protocols

Protocol Clinical Target Loading Doses Maintenance Regimen (21-Day)
POINT / CHANCE Minor Stroke (NIHSS ≤3) or High-Risk TIA (ABCD² ≥4) presenting **<24 hours** from onset. • **Aspirin:** 150-325 mg once.
• **Clopidogrel:** 300-600 mg once.		 • **Days 2–21:** Aspirin 81 mg daily + Clopidogrel 75 mg daily.
• **Days 22–90:** Aspirin 81 mg daily monotherapy.
CHANCE-2 CYP2C19 Loss-of-function carriers presenting **<24 hours**. • **Aspirin:** 150-325 mg once.
• **Ticagrelor:** 180 mg once.		 • **Days 2–21:** Aspirin 81 mg daily + Ticagrelor 90 mg BID.
• **Days 22–90:** Aspirin 81 mg daily monotherapy.
SAMMPRIS Symptomatic Large Artery Stenosis (ICAS 50-99%). • **Aspirin:** 325 mg once.
• **Clopidogrel:** 300-600 mg once.		 • **Days 2–90:** Aspirin 325 mg daily + Clopidogrel 75 mg daily.
• **Day 91+:** Aspirin 325 mg daily monotherapy.

🚨 Essential Exclusions & Contraindications

  • Prior Intracranial Hemorrhage: History of ICH, severe trauma, or active systemic bleeding.
  • Thrombolysis Candidates: Do not load DAPT prior to IV thrombolysis. Hold all antiplatelets for 24 hours post-tPA/TNK.
  • Cardioembolic Origin: AFib, mechanical valves, or thrombi require therapeutic anticoagulation, NOT long-term DAPT.
  • Severe Deficits: NIHSS ≥ 4 (unless ICAS profile) carries high hemorrhagic transformation risk if loaded with DAPT.

📈 Landmark DAPT Clinical Trials

Trial Population Intervention vs Control Efficacy Outcomes Bleeding Safety Outcomes
CHANCE (NEJM, 2013) 5,170 Chinese patients, Minor Stroke/TIA <24h. Clopidogrel (300mg load, 75mg daily) + ASA (75-300mg load, 75mg daily) for 21d vs. ASA alone. DAPT significantly reduced stroke recurrence at 90 days (8.2% vs. 11.7%, HR 0.68, p<0.001). No significant difference in moderate-to-severe bleeding (0.3% vs 0.3%).
POINT (NEJM, 2018) 4,881 international patients, Minor Stroke/TIA <24h. Clopidogrel (600mg load, 75mg daily) + ASA (50-325mg daily) for 90d vs. ASA alone. DAPT reduced major ischemic events at 90 days (5.0% vs. 6.5%, HR 0.75, p=0.02). Most benefit in first 21d. DAPT significantly increased major bleeding (0.9% vs. 0.4%, HR 2.32, p=0.02) during the 90d duration.
CHANCE-2 (NEJM, 2021) 6,431 Chinese CYP2C19 carriers, Minor Stroke/TIA <24h. Ticagrelor (180mg load, 90mg BID) + ASA vs. Clopidogrel (300mg load, 75mg daily) + ASA for 21d. Ticagrelor-DAPT reduced stroke recurrence at 90 days compared to Clopidogrel-DAPT (6.0% vs. 7.6%, HR 0.77). No difference in severe or moderate bleeding (0.3% vs 0.3%).

References: [1] Wang Y, et al. CHANCE. NEJM 2013. [2] Johnston SC, et al. POINT. NEJM 2018. [3] Wang Y, et al. CHANCE-2. NEJM 2021. [4] Chimowitz MI, et al. SAMMPRIS. NEJM 2011.
