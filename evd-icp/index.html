EVD System & Waveform Interpretation Guide

Educational Reference

🧠 What is an EVD?

A ventricular catheter placed in the lateral ventricles of the brain. It acts as a fluid column to measure Intracranial Pressure (ICP) and drains Cerebrospinal Fluid (CSF) to treat pressure elevation.

📈 Waveform Peaks & Compliance

ICP waveforms display three pulses synced to the heart beat:

  • P1 (Percussion Wave): Arterial pulsation. Normally the tallest.
  • P2 (Tidal Wave): Reflects **brain compliance** (tissue stiffness). If the brain is swelling and has no room ($P2 \ge P1$), this is an urgent compliance alarm.
  • P3 (Dicrotic Wave): Venous closure.

📏 Leveling & Zeroing

The transducer and scale 0-point must align with the **tragus (foramen of Monro)**.

  • If EVD is **too low**: Measured ICP is falsely high, driving risk of **over-drainage** (ventricle collapse, subdural hemorrhage).
  • If EVD is **too high**: Measured ICP is falsely low, leading to **under-drainage** and worsening intracranial hypertension.

💧 How CSF Drainage Works

CSF drains only when **ICP > Drip Chamber height** (in cmH2O). 1 cmH2O = 0.74 mmHg.

CSF Flow occurs if:
ICP (mmHg) > EVD Height (cmH2O) × 0.74

Stepwise ICP Escalation Pathway

Clinical Protocol
TIER 0 Baseline Neuroprotection (Prophylaxis)
Interventions: HOB elevated 30–45° • Midline neck alignment • Isotonic Saline (0.9% NS) • Maintain normothermia (T < 37.8°C) • Light sedation (Propofol/Fentanyl) • Avoid corticosteroids.
TIER 1 Basic Osmotherapy & EVD Drainage
Interventions: Open EVD to drain 5–10 mL CSF or continuous drainage (threshold 10-15 cmH2O) • 3% HTS bolus (250–500 mL IV; target Na 145–155) OR 20% Mannitol bolus (0.5–1.0 g/kg; hold if osm >320 / gap >55).
TIER 2 Invasive Rescue & Refractory Bridging
Interventions: 23.4% HTS bolus 30 mL over 10 min (CENTRAL LINE ONLY) • Short-term Hyperventilation (target PaCO2 30–35) • Escalate sedation/analgesia • Continuous EEG.
TIER 3 Surgical & Metabolic Salvage
Interventions: STAT Neurosurgery consult for decompressive hemicraniectomy or suboccipital craniectomy • Barbiturate Coma (Pentobarbital with EEG suppression target) • Mild hypothermia (32–34°C).

3. EVD Waveform & Setup Interpretation

🔑 Waveform Compliance Peaks

  • P1 (Percussion Wave): Reflects arterial pulsations. Under normal brain compliance, P1 is the tallest peak.
  • P2 (Tidal Wave): Reflects intracranial compliance (brain stiffness). If compliance is compromised (brain swelling, no space), the amplitude of P2 increases, leading to **P2 ≥ P1**. This configuration signals low compliance and risk of pressure crisis.
  • P3 (Dicrotic Wave): Reflects aortic valve closure/venous pulse.

📏 Manifold Leveling Physics

  • Tragus Alignment: The transducer and scale zero line must level to the external auditory meatus (EAM).
  • Too Low: Lowering the manifold creates a larger pressure gradient, accelerating drainage and causing falsely high ICP readings. This risks ventricles collapsing and tearing bridging veins (subdural hematoma).
  • Too High: Elevating the manifold reduces the gradient, resulting in under-drainage and falsely low ICP readings.

⚠️ Critical Safety Warning

Irrigation / Flushing: Flushing a ventricular catheter towards the patient (proximal flush) can cause catastrophic mechanical brain injury and introduces infection. Clinical teams are prohibited from flushing proximally. This must be done exclusively by Neurosurgery using sterile technique.
