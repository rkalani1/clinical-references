Stenosis Decision Helper

NASCET / ECST Criteria

Revascularization Timing Rule

Revascularization (CEA or stenting) should ideally occur within 2 weeks of the index event. Emergent intervention (<48h) is not recommended.

Revascularization Modality Selection

CEA (Carotid Endarterectomy)

Preferred first-line for patients **aged >75 years** because transfemoral stenting carries double the periprocedural stroke risk in older cohorts due to vascular tortuosity.

CAS (Carotid Artery Stenting)

Indicated if there is restenosis after a prior CEA, radiation-induced stenosis, surgically inaccessible lesions, or severe cardiac comorbidities.

TCAR (Trans-Carotid Artery Revascularization)

Combines direct carotid access with a flow reversal circuit. TCAR shows significantly lower perioperative stroke/death rates compared to transfemoral CAS.

Carotid Diagnostic Modalities

Modality Pros & Cons Overestimations
CE-MRA Highly sensitive. Contrast-enhanced is more accurate than TOF. TOF-MRA overestimates stenosis due to signal drop in turbulent flow.
CTA Accurately details calcification, soft tissue, and bony structures. Severe calcification can obscure lumen, requiring reconstruction.
Duplex U/S Fast, non-invasive bedside tool. Very operator-dependent. Can overestimate if contralateral carotid is fully occluded.
DSA (Cath) Gold standard diagnostic. 1-2% inherent complication risk. Invasive; reserved for therapeutic EVT or ambiguous non-invasive scans.

Intensive Medical Management

✓
Antiplatelets: Short-term DAPT (ASA + Clopidogrel) for minor stroke/high-risk TIA. Single agent long-term.
✓
Statin Therapy: Target LDL-C <70 mg/dL (SPARCL trial) using high-intensity statin. Add Ezetimibe if needed.
✓
Blood Pressure: Long-term SBP <130/80 mmHg. Avoid intensive diuretics initially in bilateral high-grade stenosis.
✓
Diabetes Goal: Glycemic control targeting HbA1c ≤7.0%.

🔬 Revascularization Modality Selection

  • CEA (Endarterectomy): Preferred first-line if age >75 due to tortuous femoral access.
  • CAS (Stenting): Indicated if prior CEA restenosis, neck radiation, inaccessible plaque, or high cardiac risk.
  • TCAR: Direct carotid access with reverse flow circuit. Lower stroke risk than transfemoral stenting.
  • Timing: Perform revascularization within 14 days of TIA/stroke.

💊 Intensive Medical Therapy Targets

  • Statin Goal: High-intensity statin targeting LDL-C <70 mg/dL (SPARCL trial).
  • Blood Pressure: Titrate gradually to SBP <130/80 mmHg. Avoid early dehydration in bilateral severe disease.
  • Antiplatelets: Short-term DAPT (POINT/CHANCE), then long-term single agent.
  • Diabetes: Target HbA1c ≤7.0%.

🔍 Carotid Diagnostic Modalities Comparison

Modality Clinical Advantages Limitations & Errors Sensitivity
CE-MRA Highly sensitive. Contrast-enhanced is more accurate than TOF. TOF-MRA overestimates stenosis due to flow turbulence signal loss. ~99%
CTA Accurately details calcium burden, soft tissue, and bony anatomy. Severe calcification can obscure arterial lumen. ~97%
Duplex U/S Non-invasive, fast bedside screening tool. Operator dependent. Overestimates if contralateral side is occluded. Variable
DSA Gold standard. Superior spatial resolution. Invasive with 1-2% risk of procedural stroke. 100%
