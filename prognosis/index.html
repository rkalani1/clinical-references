AHA/ASA 2022 DNR Mandate

Postpone new DNR orders until at least the second full day of inpatient stay (Class IIa, LOE C-LD).

Early care limitations are a strong predictor of poor outcomes. Early WOLST creates a "self-fulfilling prophecy" of mortality.

Clinical Framing Rule

"We are early. The brain needs time. Let us provide aggressive medical care for 72 hours, then re-evaluate the trajectory together."

Ensures families are not forced into making premature life-or-death decisions when edema peaks and recovery capacity is unknown.

Severe Acute Ischemic Stroke Prognosis

1. Early NIHSS Limitations: Immediate post-reperfusion NIHSS is not predictive of long-term functional recovery. Dramatic delayed neurological improvements (weeks to months) are common.

2. ASPECTS & Recanalization: Patients with ASPECTS ≥6 who undergo successful endovascular recanalization (TICI 2b/3) achieve functional independence (mRS 0-2) in up to 50% of cases, despite high initial NIHSS.

3. Malignant MCA Infarction: Decompressive hemicraniectomy within 48h (age <60) has an NNT of 2 for survival and 4 for functional recovery (mRS 0-3) based on DECIMAL/DESTINY/HAMLET trials.

4. Basilar Artery Occlusion: Endovascular therapy (ATTENTION/BAOCHE trials) significantly improves functional outcomes even in extended windows. Do not assume clinical futility early.

Best / Realistic / Worst Case Framework

Describe the spectrum of outcomes to manage expectations while preserving hope:

Best Case Script: "With aggressive rehab and time, he may regain the ability to walk with a cane and communicate basic needs, though some arm weakness will remain."

AHA/ASA Guidelines & Communication Templates

⚡ AHA/ASA 2022 Mandate: Postpone Early DNR

Postpone new DNR orders until at least the second full day of inpatient stay (Class IIa, LOE C-LD). Early care limitations are a strong independent predictor of poor outcomes. Early withdrawal of life-sustaining treatment (WOLST) creates a "self-fulfilling prophecy" where predicted mortality is guaranteed by withdrawing care, rather than reflecting true biological prognosis.

🧠 Acute Stroke Prognostication Pearls

  • Immediate Post-Reperfusion NIHSS: Highly unreliable indicator of recovery. Deficits often improve dramatically over weeks.
  • ASPECTS & Recanalization: ASPECTS ≥6 + successful recanalization (TICI 2b/3) leads to independence (mRS 0-2) in up to 50%.
  • Malignant MCA Decompression: Hemicraniectomy within 48h (age <60) has NNT of 2 for survival, 4 for mRS ≤3 recovery.
  • Basilar Artery Occlusion: Revascularization provides robust benefits even in extended windows. Do not declare futility early.

• Realistic Case: "He will likely require help with daily tasks and may need long-term feeding tube support."
• Worst Case: "The swelling could lead to death. If he survives, he may remain bedbound and dependent."

Key Pearls • **Avoid Subjective Labels:** Use functional descriptions instead of words like "devastating" or "catastrophic".
• **Focus on Patient Values:** Ask: "What would [Patient Name] consider an acceptable quality of life?"
• **Define What We WILL Do:** Pivot from "withdrawing care" to comfort-focused active treatments.
