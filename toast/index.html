⚡ TOAST Diagnostic Subtypes & Criteria

Subtype Category Specific Inclusion Criteria & Workup Requirements
Large-Artery Atherosclerosis (LAA) • **Clinical:** Cortical symptoms (aphasia, neglect, visual loss) or brainstem/cerebellar syndromes.
• **Imaging:** Cortical/subcortical infarct **> 1.5 cm** in diameter on CT or MRI.
• **Vascular:** **≥ 50% stenosis** or occlusion of the relevant major extracranial or large branch intracranial artery.
• **Exclusion:** Must rule out high-risk cardioembolic sources.
Small-Vessel Occlusion (SVO / Lacune) • **Clinical:** Classic lacunar syndrome (e.g. pure motor hemiparesis, pure sensory stroke) **without** cortical signs.
• **Imaging:** Normal imaging, or deep subcortical/brainstem lesion **≤ 1.5 cm** in diameter.
• **Vascular/Cardiac:** Must lack relevant large-artery ≥50% stenosis and high-risk cardioembolic sources.
Cardioembolism (CE) • **Criteria:** Patient has a high-risk or medium-risk cardiac source (see table below) with no large-artery stenosis ≥50%.
• **Cardioembolic High-Risk Sources:** Atrial fibrillation, mechanical prosthetic valves, left atrial/LAA thrombus, recent anterior MI (<4 weeks), dilated cardiomyopathy (EF <30%), infective endocarditis, sick sinus syndrome.
• **Medium-Risk Sources:** PFO + atrial septal aneurysm, mitral valve prolapse, bioprosthetic heart valves, calcific aortic stenosis, LV dysfunction (EF 30-40%), myocardial infarction (4 weeks to 6 months).
Other Determined Etiology (ODE) • **Criteria:** Stroke of any size with diagnostic proof of a rare underlying etiology:
• Arterial dissection (carotid/vertebral), CNS vasculitis, Reversible Cerebral Vasoconstriction Syndrome (RCVS), Moya-Moya disease, CADASIL, or hypercoagulable states (APLS, active malignancy, DIC).
Undetermined Etiology • **Competing Risks:** ≥2 potential causes identified (e.g., active AFib **and** ipsilateral carotid ≥50% stenosis).
• **Negative Workup (Cryptogenic):** Thorough evaluation reveals no etiology.
• **Incomplete Workup:** Required exams (Echo, vascular imaging, or ≥24h ECG) were not finished.

📋 ESUS Criteria (Embolic Stroke of Undetermined Source)

A subset of cryptogenic undetermined strokes. Patient must meet all of the following:

  • Non-lacunar ischemic stroke (cortical or large subcortical).
  • Absence of extracranial/intracranial relevant artery stenosis ≥50%.
  • No high-risk cardioembolic source (e.g., no AFib, no thrombus, EF >30%).
  • No other determined cause (e.g., dissection, vasculitis, thrombophilia).
  • Workup requires minimum CTA/MRA, TTE/TEE, and ≥24h ECG.

🔍 Required Diagnostic Triage Workup

  • Parenchymal: Brain MRI (DWI/ADC) to evaluate stroke size and location.
  • Vascular: CTA Head & Neck, MRA, or Doppler to screen for large artery disease.
  • Cardiac: Echocardiogram (TTE/TEE) to check for shunt, thrombi, or valve disease.
  • Rhythm: Continuous ECG telemetry ≥24 hours.
