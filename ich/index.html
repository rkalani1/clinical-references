Post-Lytic Intracranial Hemorrhage (ICH) Protocol

Suspicion of Intracranial Hemorrhage
Prompted by sudden neurological deterioration, new headache, acute hypertension, nausea, or vomiting
Stop thrombolytic if still running
  1. Arrange STAT non-contrast CT Head
  2. STAT blood Draw (while arranging CT Head)
    • Emergency Hemorrhage Panel (PT/INR, platelet count, fibrinogen)
    • Complete coagulation screen (PTT, TT, D-dimers)
    • CBC, type and cross
  3. Call Transfusion Support Services (4-3088 / 8-8700)
    • Order 2 pools Cryoprecipitate immediately (takes ~15-20min to obtain)
  4. Notify patient's family of change in status
CT Scan Delayed?
(i.e. if you expect results in > 30min)
Yes
Empirically administer 2 pools Cryoprecipitate over 10-30min if fibrinogen level < 200
Intracranial blood on CT Head?
Give 2 pools Cryoprecipitate infused over 10-30 min
  1. Order Tranexamic acid STAT and administer 1g IV infused over 10 min
  2. Repeat Emergency Hemorrhage Panel
  3. Call Stroke/Neurology Attending
  4. Consult Neurosurgery if needed
Update family again
  1. If labs remain abnormal on repeat testing or bleeding remains uncontrolled, consult with Hematology Attending
  2. Repeat Emergency Hemorrhagic Panel q4h until normal
Do NOT restart lytic.
End Algorithm
Consider other causes (extending infarct, seizure, hypoxia, hypercarbia, hypoglycemia, electrolytes)
*If Cryo started + infusing and CTH shows NO ICH, STOP infusion

Spontaneous Intraparenchymal Hemorrhage

Reversal Guide for Warfarin

For guideline updates and info on managing bleeds: Stroke Reference Kit Online
For all patients:
  • 1) Order STAT EMERGENCY STROKE PANEL (includes PT/INR, PTT, HB/HCT, PLT and DOAC screen)
  • 2) Obtain history about use of antithrombotic agents (especially anticoagulants), including date/time of last dose
  • 3) If crash craniotomy is considered, TYPE AND SCREEN - STAT and request 2 units emergency-release RBCs
IF ON WARFARIN
Immediately give vitamin K 10mg IV
INR ≥ 1.6
Low evidence: INR 1.3-1.5, consider on case-by-case basis
PCC (Kcentra)
  • PCC (Kcentra) 2000 units IVPB x1
  • Check PT/INR 30min, then every 6 hrs for 24 hrs after completion of PCC.
  • If INR > 1.5 at 30 min, page Hematology and consider giving another 500 units PCC.
  • If INR > 1.5 at 24 hrs, repeat vitamin K 10mg IV over 30 min.
PCC = Prothrombin Complex Concentrate; preferred agent is Kcentra
Absolute contraindications to PCC = DIC or HIT
Relative contraindications (apply to both PCC and FFP)
  • History of thrombotic or thromboembolic event in past 6 weeks (DVT/PE, ischemic stroke, ACS, acute venous/arterial ischemia, etc.)
  • Known prothrombotic condition (malignancy, hypercoagulable condition, hepatic disease, polytrauma, etc.)
  • Major surgery within 6 weeks
Plasma (FFP) Pathway (if PCC unavailable)
  1. Immediately place prepare and transfuse orders for 4 units emergency-release plasma, send a STAT type and screen, and request an additional 4 units.
    (Note: ideal plasma dose is 15mL/kg and each unit is 250-300mL, so some patients may need additional doses). Consider furosemide if patient has CHF
  2. Upon completion of infusion, repeat STAT Emergency Stroke Panel.
  3. If INR still > 1.5, give the 4 U FFP/Plasma.
  4. Upon completion, repeat STAT Emergency Stroke Panel.
  5. If INR still > 1.5 consult hematology.
  6. Repeat INR every 6 hrs for 24 hrs.
  7. If INR remains or increases to > 1.5, repeat vitamin K 10mg IV over 30 min at 24 hours and consult hematology.

Spontaneous Intraparenchymal Hemorrhage

Reversal Guide for Direct Oral Anticoagulants

Stroke Protocol September 2025
For guideline updates and info on managing bleeds: Stroke Reference Kit Online
For all patients:
  • 1) Order STAT EMERGENCY STROKE PANEL (includes PT/INR, PTT, HB/HCT, PLT and DOAC screen)
  • 2) Obtain history about use of antithrombotic agents, including date/time of last dose
  • 3) TYPE AND SCREEN - STAT
  • 4) if crash craniectomy is considered, request 2 units emergency-release RBCs
IF ON DABIGATRAN (direct thrombin inhibitor)
AND TT is prolonged or not readily available
IF ON RIVAROXABAN OR APIXABAN (factor Xa inhibitors)
AND Direct Xa inhibitor screen or anti-Xa for the specific anticoagulant (RIVAR1 or APIXN1) is elevated
IF ON EDOXABAN (factor Xa inhibitor)
Direct Xa inhibitor screen will exclude the presence of clinically relevant drug. No calibrated specific assays.
If ingestion within 2 hours, give one dose activated charcoal orally
Give idarucizumab (Praxbind) 5gm IV
administered as two 2.5gm doses no more than 15 minutes apart, each infused over 5-10 minutes
If idarucizumab is not available, proceed to consider PCC (Kcentra)
CONSIDER PCC (Kcentra) 2000 units ONLY if the patient does not have:
Absolute contraindications to PCC = DIC or HIT
Relative contraindications to PCC
  • History of thrombotic or thromboembolic event in past 6 weeks (DVT/PE, ischemic stroke, ACS, acute venous/arterial ischemia, etc.)
  • Known prothrombotic condition (malignancy, hypercoagulable condition, hepatic disease, polytrauma, etc.)
  • Major surgery within 6 weeks
Emergent dialysis may be considered in certain circumstances (renal failure; known dabigatran overdose); ~ 65% removed by hemodialysis.
Dabigatran t1/2 = 14 hrs
(up to 34 hrs in severe renal impairment)
Rivaroxaban, Apixaban & Edoxaban are NOT dialyzable
• Rivaroxaban t1/2 = 9 hrs
  (longer in renal impairment)
• Apixaban t1/2 = 12 hrs
  (longer in renal impairment)
• Edoxaban t1/2 = 10-14 hrs
  (longer in renal impairment)
PCC = Prothrombin Complex Concentrate; preferred agent is Kcentra
