⚡ Emergency Anticoagulation Reversal

Agent Lab Test First-Line Reversal Treatment
Warfarin INR **4-Factor PCC** (Kcentra): Dose by INR: INR 2-4: 25 U/kg; INR 4-6: 35 U/kg; INR >6: 50 U/kg (max 5000 U) + **Vitamin K** 10 mg IV.
Dabigatran aPTT / dTT **Idarucizumab** (Praxbind) 5 g IV (two 2.5 g vials). Alternate: aPCC (FEIBA) 50 U/kg.
Apixaban / Rivaroxaban Anti-Xa **Andexanet Alfa** (Andexxa): Low dose (400mg bolus + 4mg/min infusion for 2h) or High dose (800mg bolus + 8mg/min infusion for 2h) depending on drug dose/timing. Alternate: 4-factor PCC 50 U/kg.
Heparin (UFH) aPTT **Protamine Sulfate:** 1 mg per 100 U UFH given in past 2-3h (max 50 mg).

📉 Acute Blood Pressure Control

  • BP Target: Target **SBP <140 mmHg** (range 130-140 mmHg) per INTERACT2. Avoid dropping SBP <120 mmHg (causes renal injury per ATACH-II).
  • Acute Management Timeline: Initiate treatment within 2 hours of symptom onset. Achieve SBP target within 1 hour.
  • First-Line Options: Nicardipine IV infusion (5-15 mg/h) or Clevidipine IV infusion (1-21 mg/h).

📋 Volume Estimation & ICP Protocol

  • ABC/2 Hemorrhage Volume:
    • **A:** Maximum length on axial CT slice (cm)
    • **B:** Width perpendicular to A on same slice (cm)
    • **C:** Number of slices where blood is seen multiplied by slice thickness (cm)
  • ICP Management: Elevate head of bed to 30°, maintain neutral neck, provide sedation, hyperventilation (transient only), hyperosmolar therapy (3% saline or Mannitol).

References: [1] Hemphill JC 3rd, et al. Stroke 2001;32:891-897. [2] INTERACT2 Investigators. NEJM 2013. [3] ATACH-II Investigators. NEJM 2016. [4] 2022 AHA/ASA Guideline for the Management of Spontaneous Intracerebral Hemorrhage.
