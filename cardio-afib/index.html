Cardio & Atrial Fibrillation in Stroke

Bedside guidelines for anticoagulation in special AFib populations, left atrial appendage occlusion, and post-ICH antithrombotic resumption.

AFib: Management in Special Populations

Special Population Guideline Directive & Trial Evidence Anticoagulation Choice
Valvular AFib
(Mechanical Valves or Rheumatic Mitral Stenosis)		 DOACs are **contraindicated** (Mechanical Valves) or inferior (Mitral Stenosis). **INVICTUS Trial (2022)**: Warfarin had lower stroke and death rates than rivaroxaban in rheumatic MS. **PROACT Xa**: Apixaban failed vs. warfarin. Warfarin only (Target INR per valve type)
Bioprosthetic Heart Valves **RIVER Trial (2020)**: Rivaroxaban was non-inferior to warfarin for major clinical events at 12 months in patients with bioprosthetic mitral valves and AFib. DOAC (Apixaban/Rivaroxaban) or Warfarin
ESRD on Dialysis DOAC registration trials excluded dialysis. Observational registries show apixaban 2.5mg BID or 5mg BID has similar or lower bleeding risk than warfarin. Apixaban (dose adjusted) or Warfarin
Post-PCI (Coronary Stent) Double therapy (DOAC + P2Y12 inhibitor) significantly reduces major hemorrhage and intracranial bleeding compared to triple therapy (DOAC + DAPT). DOAC + Clopidogrel (Hold Aspirin after discharge)

Left Atrial Appendage Occlusion (LAAO) & ICH Resumption

LAA Occlusion (LAAO) Evidence • **Percutaneous LAAO (Watchman)**: Evaluated in **PROTECT AF** and **PREVAIL** trials. Watchman is non-inferior to warfarin for stroke prevention, with lower long-term bleeding rates but carries procedural and device-related thrombosis risks (2–5%).
• **Surgical LAAO (LAAOS-III, 2021)**: Performed during cardiac surgery. Surgical occlusion **significantly reduced ischemic stroke or systemic embolism by 33%** (HR 0.67, p=0.001) in patients with AFib, even when anticoagulation was continued.
Anticoagulation Resumption After ICH • **ICH Recurrence Risks**:
  – Cerebral Amyloid Angiopathy (CAA / Lobar): **~9% / year**
  – Hypertensive (Deep): **<2% / year**
• **Antiplatelet (RESTART Trial, 2019)**: Restarting antiplatelet therapy after ICH is safe and does not increase recurrent ICH.
• **Anticoagulation (OAC)**: Resumption timing is generally **~8 weeks** post-ICH if the thromboembolic risk is high (deep deep location, high CHA2DS2-VASc). Enroll in **ASPIRE** clinical trial.
