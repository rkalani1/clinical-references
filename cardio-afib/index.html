Cardio & AFib in Stroke

Anticoagulation in special populations, Watchman evidence, and post-ICH resumption

Special AFib Populations

Guideline Selector

Valvular Anticoagulation Mandate

DOACs are strictly **contraindicated** in patients with mechanical heart valves or moderate-to-severe rheumatic mitral stenosis. Warfarin is the only approved therapy.

Left Atrial Appendage Occlusion (LAAO)

Percutaneous LAAO (Watchman)

Evaluated in **PROTECT AF** and **PREVAIL** trials. Watchman is non-inferior to warfarin for stroke prevention, with lower long-term bleeding rates but carries procedural and device-related thrombosis risks (2–5%).

Surgical LAAO (LAAOS-III Trial, 2021)

Performed during concurrent cardiac surgery. Surgical occlusion **significantly reduced ischemic stroke or systemic embolism by 33%** (HR 0.67, p=0.001) in patients with AFib, even when anticoagulation was continued.

Anticoagulation Resumption After ICH

Recurrence Risks based on Location

  • Lobar ICH (Cerebral Amyloid Angiopathy): Recurrence rate is **~9% per year**. High caution with OAC.
  • Deep ICH (Hypertensive Vasculopathy): Recurrence rate is **<2% per year**. Safe to resume.

Key Trial Digests

• **RESTART (2019)**: Restarting antiplatelet therapy after ICH is safe and does not increase recurrent ICH.
• **OAC Resumption**: Generally target **~8 weeks** post-ICH if thromboembolic risk is high (deep location, high CHA2DS2-VASc). Consider enrollment in **ASPIRE** trial.

Anticoagulation in Special Populations & Post-ICH Resumption

📋 AFib Management in Special Populations

Special Population Guideline Directive & Trial Evidence Anticoagulation Choice
Valvular AFib (Mechanical Valves or Rheumatic MS) DOACs are **contraindicated** (Mechanical) or inferior (MS). **INVICTUS Trial (2022)**: Warfarin had lower stroke and death rates than rivaroxaban in rheumatic MS. Warfarin only (Target INR per valve type)
Bioprosthetic Heart Valves **RIVER Trial (2020)**: Rivaroxaban was non-inferior to warfarin for clinical events at 12 months in patients with bioprosthetic mitral valves. DOAC or Warfarin
ESRD on Dialysis Trials excluded dialysis. Observational registries show apixaban 2.5mg BID or 5mg BID has similar/lower bleeding risk than warfarin. Apixaban (dose-adjusted) or Warfarin
Post-PCI (Coronary Stent) Double therapy (DOAC + P2Y12 inhibitor) significantly reduces major hemorrhage and intracranial bleeding compared to triple therapy (DOAC + DAPT). DOAC + Clopidogrel (Hold Aspirin)

🔬 Left Atrial Appendage Occlusion (LAAO)

  • Percutaneous LAAO (Watchman): Non-inferior to warfarin for stroke prevention in PROTECT AF / PREVAIL, with lower long-term bleeding. Device thrombosis rate 2-5%.
  • Surgical LAAO (LAAOS-III): Surgical occlusion during cardiac surgery **reduced stroke or systemic embolism by 33%** (HR 0.67, p=0.001) in AFib, even with OAC.

🔴 Anticoagulation Resumption After ICH

  • Lobar ICH (CAA): Recurrence rate is **~9% per year**. High caution with OAC resumption.
  • Deep ICH (Hypertensive): Recurrence rate is **<2% per year**. Generally safe to resume.
  • Antiplatelet (RESTART): Restarting antiplatelet therapy after ICH is safe and does not increase recurrent ICH.
  • OAC Timing: Resume around **8 weeks** post-ICH in high-risk (deep location, high CHA2DS2-VASc). Enroll in ASPIRE.

References: [1] INVICTUS. N Engl J Med. 2022. [2] RIVER. N Engl J Med. 2020. [3] LAAOS-III. N Engl J Med. 2021. [4] RESTART. Lancet. 2019.
