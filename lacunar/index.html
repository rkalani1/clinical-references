Classic Lacunar Syndromes

Localization Helper

Capsular Warning Syndrome

Recurrent, brief motor TIAs that represent progressive microthrombosis in penetrating branches. Consider brief hemodynamic support; avoid routine early long-term DAPT.

Vascular Pathologies & Genetics

Microvascular Pathology

  • Arteriolosclerosis (40–150 μm): Luminal narrowing and media thickening driven by chronic HTN and aging.
  • Lipohyalinosis (40–200 μm): Fibrinoid necrosis and disorganized collagen deposition in small arteries.
  • Branch Atheromatous Disease (BAD) (200–800 μm): Microatheroma at the ostium or proximal segment of a larger branch.

Genetics & Rare SVD

  • CADASIL: AD NOTCH3 mutation. Subcortical white matter hyperintensities (temporal pole hallmark), migraines, and early dementia.
  • CARASIL: AR HTRA1 mutation. Characterized by early alopecia, severe spondylosis, and cognitive decline.
  • RVCL-S: AD TREX1 mutation. Retinopathy, progressive leukodystrophy, and systemic vasculopathy (Raynaud's, liver cirrhosis).

Bedside Care Targets for Small Vessel Disease

✓
BP Target: Maintain long-term SBP <130 mmHg (reduces ICH risk by 63%!).
✓
Statin Goal: High-intensity statin targeting LDL-C <70 mg/dL (SPARCL trial).
✓
Antiplatelet Rule: Single antiplatelet agent (aspirin 81mg or clopidogrel 75mg) long-term. Avoid long-term DAPT.
✓
Lifestyle Targets: Mediterranean diet, sodium restriction, and daily aerobic exercise.

🔬 SPS3 Trial Evidence: Antiplatelet & BP Targets

SPS3 Antiplatelet Arm (DAPT vs. ASA):

• Recurrent stroke was **not** significantly reduced (HR 0.92, 95% CI 0.72–1.16).
• DAPT significantly increased **major hemorrhage** and **mortality** (HR 1.52, p<0.001).
• Takeaway: Routine long-term DAPT is contraindicated for lacunes; use single antiplatelet.

SPS3 Blood Pressure Arm (Target <130 vs. 130-149):

• Recurrent stroke: 19% reduction (HR 0.81, 95% CI 0.64–1.03), trending toward benefit.
• **Intracranial Hemorrhage:** **Highly significantly reduced** (HR 0.37, 95% CI 0.15–0.95), showing massive protection.
• Takeaway: Target SBP <130 mmHg long-term.

🧬 Pathophysiology & Rare Genetic Etiologies

Vascular Mechanisms:

  • Arteriolosclerosis / Lipohyalinosis: Chronically high SBP drives fibrinoid necrosis in 40–200 μm branches.
  • Branch Atheromatous Disease (BAD): Microatheroma at the ostium of penetrating arteries (200-800 μm).

Rare Genotypes:

  • CADASIL: AD NOTCH3 mutation. Halo white matter on MRI (temporal pole hallmark), migraines, and early dementia.
  • CARASIL: AR HTRA1 mutation. Alopecia + spondylosis.
  • RVCL-S: AD TREX1. Retinopathy + progressive dementia.
