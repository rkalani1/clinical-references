Epidemiology & Genetic Screening

Classification & Distribution

• **Saccular (Berry):** Thin media, fragmented elastic lamina. Most common cause of SAH.
• **Fusiform:** Circumferential dilatation, highly linked to atherosclerosis.
• **CN III Warning:** PCom aneurysms compressing the nerve can cause pupil-involving third nerve palsy (surgical emergency).

Genetic Screening Mandates

• Screen all patients with **≥2 first-degree relatives** with aneurysms/SAH.
• Screen patients with genetic syndromes such as **AD-PCKD (7x risk)** or Ehlers-Danlos.

Untreated Aneurysm Surveillance Schedule

Imaging Modality Accuracy

• **MRA / CTA:** Variable accuracy for very small lesions (<4mm). 3T TOF-MRA (Sens 99%, Spec 94%) or CTA (Sens 97%, Spec 98%) preferred.
• **DSA (Gold Standard):** Best resolution. Procedural complication risk is ~1-2%.

Recommended Re-imaging Intervals

  • Obtain follow-up MRA or CTA **6 months** after initial diagnosis.
  • If stable, repeat **annually** for 2–3 years.
  • If stable, transition to **q2–3 years** or longer.

Clipping vs. Coiling Guidelines

General Indications for Intervention

• Symptomatic aneurysms (mass effect, cranial neuropathy) or large size (**≥7–10 mm**).

• Basilar tip location, young patient age, or documented growth on serial scans.

👁️ CN III Compression Alert

Aneurysms near the PCom junction can cause mass effect, presenting as a **pupil-involving third nerve palsy**. This represents a clinical surgical emergency indicating micro-rupture or acute expansion.

🛡️ Surveillance Scan Intervals

  • 6 Months: Repeat non-invasive imaging (MRA/CTA) at 6 months post-initial diagnosis.
  • Annual: Scan annually for 2–3 years if stable.
  • Extended: Transition to q2–3 years thereafter if no growth.
  • Aspirin: Clinically safe; reduces wall inflammation.

🛠️ Intervention Indications (Clipping vs. Coiling)

Intervention Target Clinical Criteria & Treatment Choice
Absolute Indications Symptomatic aneurysms (cranial neuropathy, mass effect) or large size (≥ 7–10 mm).
Relative Indications Young patient age, basilar tip location, family history of SAH, or documented growth on serial scans.
Modality Choice Endovascular (coiling, flow diversion) has lower perioperative risks. Open surgery (clipping) has lower recurrence rates.
