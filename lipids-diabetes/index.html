Lipids & Diabetes Control in Stroke

Atherosclerotic risk factor targets, metabolic biomarkers, and acute glycemic control evidence.

Lipid Management & Statin Targets

Lipid-Stroke Duality & Targets • **Ischemic Stroke**: Strong positive association between elevated total cholesterol / LDL-C and large artery atherosclerosis and small vessel disease.
• **Hemorrhagic Stroke**: **Inverse association**. Extremely low total cholesterol & LDL-C values are associated with an increased risk of intracerebral hemorrhage (ICH) and lobar hemorrhage.
• **Treatment Target**: LDL-C <70 mg/dL using high-intensity statins (Atorvastatin 80mg) ± Ezetimibe ± PCSK9i.
• **SPARCL Trial (2006)**: Secondary prevention stroke patients. Atorvastatin 80mg reduced recurrent stroke rate by 16% (HR 0.84, p=0.03).
Emerging Lipid Biomarkers • **Apolipoprotein B (ApoB)**: Measures total number of atherogenic particles (LDL, VLDL, IDL). Better predictor of cardiovascular events than LDL-C in statin-treated cohorts.
• **Apolipoprotein A1 (ApoA1)**: Primary structural protein of HDL. Reflects reverse cholesterol transport.
• **Lipoprotein (a) [Lp(a)]**: Genetically determined particle. Highly atherogenic and prothrombotic.
• **Lp-PLA2**: Biomarker of vascular inflammation within the atherosclerotic plaque itself.

Diabetes Mellitus & Acute Hyperglycemia

Metabolic Targets & Screening • **Stroke Risk**: Diabetes increases ischemic stroke risk 2-fold (higher in women, longer duration).
• **Prediabetes**: Impaired fasting glucose or HbA1c 5.7–6.4% increases risk.
• **Insulin Resistance**: Insulin resistance (HOMA-IR >3) increases risk even in non-diabetic patients.
• **Long-term Target**: HbA1c <7.0% for secondary stroke prevention (Class I, LOE A). Screen post-stroke patients with fasting plasma glucose or HbA1c.
SHINE Trial (Acute Hyperglycemia) • **Design**: RCT in acute stroke hyperglycemic patients. Intensive IV Insulin (target 80–130 mg/dL) vs. Standard subq sliding scale insulin (target 80–179 mg/dL) for 72 hours.
• **Efficacy**: **No difference** in 90-day functional outcomes (mRS 0-2 shift).
• **Harm**: Intensive group had **highly significantly higher severe hypoglycemia** (glucose <40 mg/dL: 11.2% vs. 0%).
• **Clinical Takeaway**: Use standard subq sliding scale (target <180 mg/dL) in acute phase. Avoid intensive IV infusion.
