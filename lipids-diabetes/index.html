Secondary Prevention Targets

Secondary Prevention
Recommended Targets
Target LDL-C: <70 mg/dL
Target HbA1c: <7.0%

Lipid-Stroke Duality warning

In hemorrhagic stroke (ICH), extremely low total cholesterol & LDL-C levels are associated with increased recurrence risks. Use high-dose statins with caution unless there is strong concomitant CAD.

Lipid Management & Biomarkers

SPARCL Trial (2006)

In patients with recent TIA or stroke and no history of coronary artery disease, Atorvastatin 80mg daily reduced the relative risk of recurrent stroke by 16% (HR 0.84, p=0.03).

Emerging Biomarkers

  • Apolipoprotein B (ApoB): Measures total atherogenic particles; better cardiac predictor than LDL-C in statin-treated cohorts.
  • Apolipoprotein A1 (ApoA1): Measures structural HDL; reflects reverse cholesterol transport.
  • Lipoprotein (a) [Lp(a)]: Genetically determined particle. Highly atherogenic and prothrombotic.
  • Lp-PLA2: Biomarker of vascular inflammation within the plaque.

Diabetes Mellitus & SHINE Trial Guidelines

SHINE Trial (Acute Glycemia)

In acute ischemic stroke, intensive IV insulin therapy (target 80–130 mg/dL) showed no difference in 90-day functional recovery compared to standard subq sliding scale insulin (target 80–179 mg/dL).

However, the intensive group experienced severe hypoglycemia (11.2% vs. 0% standard). Use standard sliding scale (target <180 mg/dL) in acute stroke.

Metabolic Targets & Screening

Screen post-stroke patients with fasting plasma glucose or HbA1c. Long-term target is HbA1c <7.0% for secondary stroke prevention (Class I, LOE A).

Lipids & Diabetes Control in Stroke

Metabolic Risk Factor Targets & SHINE Trial Evidence Card

🔬 Lipid-Stroke Duality & Targets

  • Ischemic Stroke Target: LDL-C <70 mg/dL using high-intensity statins (Atorvastatin 80mg daily) ± Ezetimibe.
  • Hemorrhagic Stroke Warning: Inverse relationship. Very low total cholesterol and LDL-C are associated with increased lobar and deep ICH recurrence.
  • SPARCL Trial (2006): Atorvastatin 80mg daily reduced recurrent stroke rate by 16% (HR 0.84, p=0.03) in secondary prevention.

🩸 Emerging Lipid Biomarkers

  • Apolipoprotein B (ApoB): Measures total count of all atherogenic particles (LDL, VLDL, IDL). Better cardiovascular predictor than LDL-C in statin cohorts.
  • Apolipoprotein A1 (ApoA1): Primary structural protein of HDL; reflects reverse cholesterol transport.
  • Lipoprotein (a) [Lp(a)]: Genetically determined particle. Highly atherogenic and prothrombotic.
  • Lp-PLA2: Biomarker of active vascular wall inflammation.

📋 Diabetes & Metabolic Targets

  • Secondary Prevention: Target HbA1c <7.0% for all ischemic stroke patients (Class I, LOE A).
  • Screening: Obtain fasting plasma glucose or HbA1c in all post-stroke/TIA patients who do not have a prior history of diabetes.
  • Prediabetes Risk: HbA1c 5.7–6.4% or insulin resistance (HOMA-IR >3) significantly increases stroke risk.

⚡ SHINE Trial: Acute Glycemic Target

Design: RCT comparing intensive IV insulin (target 80-130 mg/dL) vs. standard subcutaneous sliding scale (target 80-179 mg/dL) for 72h acute phase.
Efficacy: No difference in 90-day functional independence (mRS 0-2).
Harm: Severe hypoglycemia (glucose <40 mg/dL) occurred in 11.2% of intensive group vs. 0% standard.
Takeaway: Use standard subcutaneous sliding scale insulin (target <180 mg/dL) in acute phase; avoid intensive IV.

References: [1] SPARCL. N Engl J Med. 2006. [2] SHINE. JAMA. 2019. [3] AHA/ASA Primary & Secondary Prevention Guidelines.
