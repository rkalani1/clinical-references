⚡ Stroke Severity Timing Grid (ELAN 2023)

Severity (NIHSS) Imaging / Core Definition Repeat Imaging DOAC Start Day
TIA / Mild Stroke (NIHSS <8) Core **< 1.5 cm** in cortex or deep structures. Not routinely required prior to DOAC start. **Day 1 or 2**
Moderate Stroke (NIHSS 8-15) Single superficial MCA/PCA/ACA branch occlusion. Repeat Head CT on **Day 2–3** to exclude hemorrhage. **Day 3 or 4**
Severe Stroke (NIHSS ≥16) Multilobar core, main MCA stem trunk occlusion. Repeat Head CT on **Day 5–6** to exclude hemorrhage. **Day 6 or 7**
Hemorrhagic Conversion Parenchymal Hematoma (PH-2) on repeat scans. Obtain follow-up imaging at Day 10–12. **Day 12, 13, or 14**

🚨 DOAC Dosing & Dose Reduction Criteria

Drug Standard Dosing Dose Reduction Guidelines
Apixaban (Eliquis) 5 mg orally twice daily **Reduce to 2.5 mg BID** if ≥ 2 criteria met: Age ≥80 years, Weight ≤60 kg, or Serum creatinine ≥1.5 mg/dL.
Rivaroxaban (Xarelto) 20 mg orally daily (with food) **Reduce to 15 mg daily** if CrCl is 15–50 mL/min.
Hold if CrCl < 15 mL/min.
Dabigatran (Pradaxa) 150 mg orally twice daily **Reduce to 75 mg BID** if CrCl is 15–30 mL/min.
Avoid if CrCl < 15 mL/min.
Edoxaban (Savaysa) 60 mg orally daily **Reduce to 30 mg daily** if CrCl is 15–50 mL/min or weight ≤60 kg.
Avoid if CrCl >95 mL/min (increased clearance).

📈 Landmark Anticoagulation Initiation Evidence

Clinical Study Cohort Inclusions & Timing Strategies Major Efficacy & Bleeding Safety Findings
ELAN (NEJM, 2023) 2,013 patients with AFib and recent stroke. Early DOAC (Day 1-2 for mild, 3-4 moderate, 6-7 severe) vs. Delayed DOAC (Day 3-4 mild, 6-7 moderate, 12-14 severe). Early DOAC was safe and non-inferior to delayed DOAC. Recurrent stroke rates were lower in early group (1.9% vs 3.1% at 30d). sICH was identical (0.6% vs 0.7%).
OPTIMAS / TIMING (Lancet, 2024 / Circ, 2022) Pooled meta-analyses of early vs late anticoagulation. TIMING: Day ≤4 vs Day 5-10. OPTIMAS: Day ≤4 vs Day 7-14. Confirmed zero excess risk of hemorrhage with early initiation. Strongly supports starting DOACs in first week. DOACs are preferred over Warfarin.

References: [1] Fischer U, et al. ELAN. NEJM 2023. [2] Fischer U, et al. OPTIMAS. Lancet 2024. [3] Oldgren J, et al. TIMING. Circulation 2022. [4] 2023 ACC/AHA Atrial Fibrillation Guidelines.
