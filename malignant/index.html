Decompressive Hemicraniectomy & ICU Supportive Protocols

⚡ Decompressive Hemicraniectomy Selection Criteria

  • Clinical Deficit Severity:
    • NIHSS >15 in non-dominant hemisphere stroke.
    • NIHSS >20 in dominant hemisphere stroke.
    • **AND** decrease in level of consciousness (NIHSS Item 1a score ≥ 1 / drowsy or stuporous).
  • Radiographic Markers (CT or MRI):
    • Infarction of ≥ 50% of the MCA territory.
    • DWI core volume > 82 mL within 6 hours of onset.
    • DWI core volume > 145 mL within 14 hours of onset.
  • Timing: Surgery must be performed **within 48 hours** of onset.
  • Surgical Spec: Bone flap diameter **≥ 12–15 cm** with duraplasty.

🚨 Supportive ICU Care & Medical Management

Protocol Area Standard Intervention Criteria
HOB Elevation Elevate HOB **30 degrees** with head/neck kept in neutral alignment to maximize jugular venous drainage and reduce intracranial pressure.
Fluids Maintain euvolemia using **Isotonic Saline (0.9% NS)**. Avoid hypotonic fluids (D5W, 0.45% NS, LR) which worsen cytotoxic edema.
Osmotherapy Targeted **PRN** hyperosmolar therapy (HTS 3% or Mannitol 20%) for acute clinical pupil changes or severe midline shift. Avoid prophylactic use.
Metabolic Target Target normothermia (<37.8°C). Target normocapnia (PaCO2 35-45 mmHg); avoid hypoventilation which raises SBP/ICP.
Steroids **Class III (Harmful):** Glucocorticoids are contraindicated for cerebral edema in ischemic stroke. Do not use.

📊 Hemicraniectomy Outcomes & Evidence Summary

Age Category Core Evidence Trials Survival Benefit (Mortality) Functional Outcomes (mRS ≤ 3) Clinical Takeaway
Age < 60 Years DECIMAL, DESTINY, HAMLET pooled meta-analysis **22%** with Surgery vs **71%** with Medical Management (ARR 49%, NNT = 2 for survival). **43%** with Surgery vs **21%** with Medical Management (NNT = 4 for functional independence). Highly recommended. Strong survival and functional independence benefit. Perform surgery <48 hours.
Age ≥ 60 Years DESTINY II Trial (NEJM 2014) **33%** with Surgery vs **70%** with Medical Management (ARR 37%, NNT = 3 for survival). **7%** with Surgery vs **3%** with Medical Management (NNT = 25 for functional independence). Significant survival benefit but functional recovery is limited (most survivors have mRS 4 or 5). Strict counseling and goals-of-care discussion critical.

References: [1] Vahedi K, et al. DECIMAL. Stroke 2007. [2] Jüttler E, et al. DESTINY. Stroke 2007. [3] Jüttler E, et al. DESTINY II. NEJM 2014. [4] Wijdicks EF, et al. AHA Guidelines. Stroke 2014.
