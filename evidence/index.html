Critical Appraisal Framework

Superiority RCTs
Step 1

Baseline Prognostic Balance

Verify random computerized allocation and allocation concealment to prevent recruitment selection bias.

Step 2

Maintained Prognostic Balance

Check double-blinding (patients, care teams, outcome assessors) to prevent performance and detection biases.

Step 3

Intention-to-Treat (ITT)

Analyze crossover/withdrawn patients in their randomized groups to preserve initial prognostic balance.

Step 4

Precision & Magnitude

Calculate ARR and NNT to evaluate impact. Check confidence intervals for statistical precision.

Evidence-Based Medicine (EBM) Bedside Guide

Superiority Trial Appraisal Checklist & Critical Stroke Trial Summary

📋 RCT Critical Appraisal Checklist (Superiority Design)

Appraisal Domain Critical Evaluation Checklist Questions & Bedside Practice
1. Prognostic Balance • Was the randomization computer-generated and truly unpredictable?
• Was allocation concealed (e.g. centralized phone or web allocation) to prevent investigator selection bias?
• Were baseline characteristics statistically comparable in Table 1?
2. Blinding & Maintenance • Were patients, clinical care teams, and outcome assessors blinded to prevent performance/detection biases?
• Were co-interventions (e.g. critical care, drugs) standardized and balanced between the treatment arms?
3. Analysis Integrity • Was **Intention-to-Treat (ITT)** used? Were crossed-over or withdrawn patients analyzed in their randomized groups?
• Did follow-up exceed 80-90%? Verify that attrition rates did not introduce systematic drop-out bias.
4. Magnitude & Precision • Calculate ARR: **|Control Risk - Treatment Risk|** and NNT: **1 / ARR**.
• Assess precision of results: Check if the 95% Confidence Interval (CI) spans the line of no-effect (1.0 for HR/OR, 0 for ARR).

🔬 EBM Calculation Reference

Metric Name Formula / Calculation
Absolute Risk Red (ARR) ARR = |CER - EER|
Relative Risk Red (RRR) RRR = ARR / CER
Number Needed to Treat (NNT) NNT = 1 / ARR (Round up)
Number Needed to Harm (NNH) NNH = 1 / ARI (Absolute Risk Increase)

🧠 Key Stroke Trials High-Yield Summary

  • SPARCL (Atorvastatin): Secondary prevention ischemic stroke. Atorvastatin 80mg daily reduces recurrent stroke by 16% (HR 0.84, NNT = 46).
  • SHINE (Acute Glycemia): Standard subcutaneous sliding scale (target <180) is equal to intensive IV insulin (80-130) but avoids severe hypoglycemia.
  • SPS3 (Small Vessel BP): Target SBP <130 mmHg reduces intracranial hemorrhage risk by 63% (HR 0.37). DAPT is harmful long-term.
  • LAAOS-III (LAA Occlusion): Surgical LAA occlusion during cardiac surgery reduces systemic emboli by 33% (HR 0.67).
  • ELAN (DOAC Start): Early DOAC start (Day 1–2 mild, Day 3–4 moderate, Day 6–7 severe) is safe and preferred over late resumption.
