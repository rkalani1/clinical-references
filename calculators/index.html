The standard score for measuring the degree of disability or dependence in daily activities.

No symptoms
Entirely normal state, no limitations or complaints.
1

Clinical Calculators Cheat Sheet

Pocket Reference for Neurology & Stroke Scores

🧠 GCS (Glasgow Coma Scale)

  • Eyes (1-4): 4: Spontaneous, 3: To speech, 2: To pain, 1: None
  • Verbal (1-5): 5: Oriented, 4: Confused, 3: Inappropriate words, 2: Incomprehensible sounds, 1: None
  • Motor (1-6): 6: Obeys, 5: Localizes, 4: Withdraws, 3: Flexion (decorticate), 2: Extension (decerebrate), 1: None

⚡ HAS-BLED (Bleeding Risk in AFib)

Score ≥ 3 indicates high bleeding risk; caution advised.

  • H (1): Hypertension (SBP ≥ 160)
  • A (1): Abnormal renal or liver function (1 pt each, max 2)
  • S (1): Stroke history
  • B (1): Bleeding predisposition / history
  • L (1): Labile INRs (high variability, time in range < 60%)
  • E (1): Elderly (Age ≥ 65)
  • D (1): Drugs (antiplatelets/NSAIDs) or Alcohol (1 pt each, max 2)

📋 CHA2DS2-VASc (Stroke Risk in AFib)

  • C (1): Congestive Heart Failure / LV dysfunction
  • H (1): Hypertension history
  • A2 (2): Age ≥ 75 years
  • D (1): Diabetes Mellitus
  • S2 (2): Stroke, TIA, or thromboembolism history
  • V (1): Vascular disease (prior MI, PAD, aortic plaque)
  • A (1): Age 65 - 74 years
  • Sc (1): Sex category (Female)

🔍 RCVS2 (Reversible Cerebral Vasoconstriction)

  • R (5): Recurrent thunderclap headaches (TCHs)
  • C (1): Carotid / vertebral artery dissection
  • V (1): Vasoconstrictor trigger identified
  • S (1): Sex category (Female)
  • 2 (-2): Subarachnoid hemorrhage present (**minus 2 points**)

Score ≥ 5: High probability of RCVS (~99% specificity).

📈 mRS (Modified Rankin Scale) for Functional Outcomes

mRS Score Clinical Description
0No symptoms at all.
1No significant disability; able to carry out all usual duties and activities.
2Slight disability; unable to carry out all previous activities, but able to look after own affairs without assistance.
3Moderate disability; requiring some help, but able to walk without assistance.
4Moderately severe disability; unable to walk without assistance and unable to attend to own bodily needs without assistance.
5Severe disability; bedridden, incontinent and requiring constant nursing care and attention.
6Dead.
